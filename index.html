About

I came to UX from a decade in print production. I learned color and precision on a press before I ever designed a screen, then joined Kyocera in 2005 and have led product design ever since, from phones and TVs to payment terminals, medical devices and clinical data.

I care about how products sound, not just how they look. I shaped product sound on the Kyocera Rio and Verifone's terminals.

I've mentored more than 200 designers through CareerFoundry. Today I consult as John Salisbury Design, from Fallbrook, California and Washington, DC.
